# Bought together, AI-slop review of the built page, round 1

Reviewed the page at `#/models/product-affinity` against `docs/design/SLOP_CHECKLIST.md`, with every exemption in its conflicts section applied, including Kilian's Gate 0 decision. Wording was checked in `frontend/src/models/affinity/copy.ts` (all surface and drawer strings, templates included). Numbers and method are out of scope.

Mechanical checks with no hits are the em dash and en dash characters, the AI vocabulary list, marketing words, copula avoidance, forced contrast, hollow "-ing" tails (the one participle, "filling FC 1, then FC 2", states a fact), signposting, wrap-ups, vague attribution, chatbot residue, placeholders, emoji, Title Case and colons in prose. No page string sits outside `copy.ts`.

On the design side, there are no gradients, gradient text, shadows or rounded corners on this page. The only gradients in the CSS are the clustering page's diverging legend and the simulation map background. There are no kickers, icon tiles, side stripes, glow or decorative motion. Rotation starts off under `prefers-reduced-motion` (`motion.ts`, used on both pages). Indigo marks only our result, plus the house drawer icon and the pressed and selected states. Magnitudes use the blue stepped scale, the histogram is grey, and the scatter has direct labels and no legend box. Community colours carry a text label in the side panel. The FC squares have hover, keyboard focus (a dashed ring replaces the removed outline) and community outlines. All round 1 and round 2 spec findings appear fixed in the build, except the screen 4 caption (row 2).
