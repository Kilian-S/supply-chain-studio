# AI slop checklist

A checklist of recognisable signs of AI-generated writing and design, drawn from the sources listed at the end.
Critics use it to review each screen, headline, caption and drawer of the model showcase pages, item by item.

Severity says how strongly a sign marks a page as machine-made. High means one instance on a screen is enough to fail it. Medium means it fails when repeated or combined with another sign. Low means fix it when seen. Rows are ordered by severity.

## Writing

| Sign | How to spot it | Fix | Severity | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forced contrast (negative parallelism) | Search for "not X, but Y", "It's not X, it's Y", "not just X, but also Y", "not only ... but also", "X rather than Y", "no X, no Y, just Z", "isn't about X, it's about Y". Any one in a headline or caption counts. | State the positive claim directly ("Demand clusters by volatility"). Drop the straw man. | High | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [Washington Post via Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/11/13/business/chatgpt-writing-style-clues/) |
| Em dash habit | Search the visible strings and the lexicon for the em dash character U+2014 (also " - " used as a dash between clauses). | Use a full stop, comma or parentheses. | High | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), [Washington Post via Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/11/13/business/chatgpt-writing-style-clues/), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Inflated significance | Phrases such as "plays a crucial/pivotal/key role", "is a testament to", "underscores the importance of", "sets the stage for", "marks a turning point", "reflects broader trends", "leaves an indelible mark", "this distinction matters because". | Say what the result is and what number shows it. Let the chart carry the weight. | High | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| AI vocabulary density | Count these per screen or drawer (any spelling). delve, underscore, showcase, pivotal, crucial, intricate, tapestry, landscape (abstract), meticulous, garner, interplay, leverage, multifaceted, foster, robust, seamless, holistic, nuanced, realm, core, potential, findings, additionally. Two or more on one screen fails. | Replace with the plain word or cut. "Findings" becomes the finding itself. | High | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), [Kobak et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07016v5), [Washington Post via Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/11/13/business/chatgpt-writing-style-clues/) |
| Hollow "-ing" tail | A sentence ends with a comma plus a present participle that adds commentary, not fact. ", highlighting ...", ", underscoring ...", ", emphasising ...", ", ensuring ...", ", reflecting ...", ", contributing to ...", ", paving the way for ...". | Delete the tail, or make it a separate sentence with a concrete claim. | High | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Marketing register | "powerful", "cutting-edge", "groundbreaking", "game-changing", "unlock", "supercharge", "empower", "streamline", "world-class", "vibrant", "rich insights", "boasts", "in the heart of", "valuable insights". | Name what the reader can see or do, with a number where possible. | High | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Invented or suspiciously round numbers | Figures like 47%, 10,000+, 99.9%, "3x faster" with no link to the data; placeholder names (Acme, John Doe, TechCorp); example numbers that do not match the chart. | Every number must come from the model output and match the chart to the shown precision. Remove any that cannot be traced. | High | [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Chatbot residue and placeholders | "Certainly!", "Great question", "I hope this helps", "Let me know", "Would you like ...", "as of my last update", "[Year]", "INSERT_", "TODO", "lorem ipsum", example.com links, "utm_source=chatgpt.com", "oaicite". | Delete. Grep the build for these strings before every review. | High | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Emoji as decoration | Any emoji in a headline, caption, list or button (common offenders ✅ 🧠 🔹 ✨ 🚀 📊 💡). | Remove. Use a plain word or the house icon set. | High | [Washington Post via Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/11/13/business/chatgpt-writing-style-clues/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Vague attribution | "Research shows", "experts argue", "studies suggest", "industry reports", "it is widely accepted", "several sources" without a named source in the drawer. | Name the source (author, year) in the drawer, or drop the claim. | High | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Caption that restates the headline | Read headline and caption together. If the caption says the same thing in more words, or a "summary" line repeats what the chart already shows, it fails. Same label repeated in one card also counts. | Cut the caption. Keep it only if it adds a fact the chart cannot show. | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Rule of three | Triads of adjectives or parallel phrases ("fast, flexible and scalable"; "cleaner data, sharper clusters and better policies"). More than one triad per page fails. | Keep the items that are true and needed. Two or four is fine. | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Copula avoidance | "serves as", "stands as", "functions as", "acts as", "represents", "features", "boasts", "offers" where "is" or "has" would do. | Use "is", "are", "has". | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Signposting and throat-clearing | "It's important to note", "It's worth noting", "Notably,", "Interestingly,", "Importantly,", "Let's dive in", "Let's explore", "In this section we ...", "Here's why", "The result?". | Start with the content. Delete the opener. | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Formulaic wrap-up | "In summary", "In conclusion", "Overall,", "Ultimately,", "Despite these challenges", "paves the way", "the future looks", a "Challenges and outlook" section. | End on the last result. Put limitations as plain facts in a drawer. | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Hedge stacks and corrective framing | "may potentially", "could possibly", "should be treated as X rather than Y", "does not establish", "should not be confused with", "not widely documented". | One hedge at most, tied to the actual uncertainty (for example a confidence interval). | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Vague connection | "associated with", "linked to", "in connection with", "tied to" where the data supports a precise relation (a correlation, a share, a difference). | State the relation and its size ("Pearson r = 0.62"). | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Bold label lists | Bullets of the form "**Label**: explanation", or bold scattered through prose for emphasis. | Write a plain sentence or a real table. Bold nothing in body text. | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Elegant variation | The same thing gets several names across screens (SKU, product, item, article; cluster, group, segment). | One term per concept, taken from the lexicon. | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Uniform staccato rhythm | Runs of short declaratives or fragments of similar length ("Fast. Simple. Accurate."), every sentence the same shape. | Vary sentence length. Join related short sentences with "and", "because", "so". | Medium | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Headline that sells instead of names | Headlines like "Unlocking hidden patterns", "The power of clustering", "From chaos to clarity", "Why X matters". | Name what is shown ("Six demand profiles across 4,000 SKUs"). | Medium | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| "Theatre" and similar dismissals | Calling an approach "theatre", "smoke and mirrors", "a silver bullet" in place of an explanation. | Explain what is wrong with it in one sentence. | Low | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Title Case Headings | Every Major Word Capitalised in headings or buttons. | Sentence case. | Low | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| Markdown or typographic leaks | Visible "**", "##", "---", backticks, "[text](url)" in rendered UI; mixed curly and straight quotes on one page. | Render properly; pick one quote style and use it everywhere. | Low | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |

## Design

| Sign | How to spot it | Fix | Severity | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purple or blue gradient | Any `linear-gradient` or `radial-gradient` from violet or indigo to blue or pink on buttons, headings, cards or page background. Grep the CSS for `gradient`. | Solid colours only. One accent, used flat. | High | [Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills), [prg.sh](https://prg.sh/ramblings/Why-Your-AI-Keeps-Building-the-Same-Purple-Gradient-Website), [Fountain Institute](https://pages.thefountaininstitute.com/posts/7-tells-that-a-ui-is-ai-generated) |
| Gradient text | `background-clip: text` with a gradient on a heading or a large number. | One solid colour. Emphasise with size or weight. | High | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Hero template | Centred headline, one-line subtitle, single call-to-action button, then a row of three equal icon cards. | Left-aligned narrative, one idea per screen, the visual leading. | High | [prg.sh](https://prg.sh/ramblings/Why-Your-AI-Keeps-Building-the-Same-Purple-Gradient-Website), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Equal-weight KPI row | Three or four identical tiles, each with a small label, a big number and a coloured delta, side by side with no primary. | Make one number primary (2 to 3 times larger) or show the numbers inside the chart. Cut tiles that do not serve the screen's idea. | High | [UX Skill dashboard wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-design-a-dashboard-with-Claude-Code), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Hero metric with no meaning | A giant number with a tiny label and no unit, baseline or comparison ("4,312"). | Give the unit and the comparison, or drop the number. | High | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Cards everywhere, cards in cards | Every block sits in a bordered or shadowed box; boxes nested inside boxes; a lone number wrapped in a card. | Remove containers. Group with spacing and hairline rules. | High | [Fountain Institute](https://pages.thefountaininstitute.com/posts/7-tells-that-a-ui-is-ai-generated), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [UX Skill dashboard wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-design-a-dashboard-with-Claude-Code) |
| Kicker, badge or number above a title | A small uppercase label, pill badge ("NEW", "Model J"), or "01 /" numbering above a headline. | Delete it. Fold anything useful into the headline. | High | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Emoji as icons | Emoji used as feature icons, bullets, nav items or chart markers. | Remove, or use the single house icon (accent circle with a white plus). | High | [Fountain Institute](https://pages.thefountaininstitute.com/posts/7-tells-that-a-ui-is-ai-generated), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Accessibility left to chance | Grey text under 4.5:1 contrast; body text under 16 px; series told apart by colour alone; `outline: none` with no `:focus-visible`; no `prefers-reduced-motion`. | Check contrast, add direct labels to series, keep visible focus, honour reduced motion. | High | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design), [prg.sh](https://prg.sh/ramblings/Why-Your-AI-Keeps-Building-the-Same-Purple-Gradient-Website) |
| Library-default charts | Chart.js, Plotly or Recharts default palette, boxed legend, heavy gridlines, default tooltip, chart type that does not fit the data, proportional digits in tables. | House palette, direct labels in place of a legend, light or no gridlines, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, a chart type chosen for the question. | High | [Tableau User Group](https://usergroups.tableau.com/events/details/tableau-st-louis-tableau-user-group-presents-i-can-tell-ai-built-your-dashboard-and-thats-a-problem/), [UX Skill dashboard wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-design-a-dashboard-with-Claude-Code) |
| Icon tile above every heading | A rounded square or coloured circle holding an icon above each feature or section heading. | Drop the tile. Use no icon unless it carries meaning. | Medium | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Shadow, border and radius stacked | Same element has a border, a soft shadow (around 0.1 opacity) and a large radius (`rounded-2xl`, 16 px or more). | Pick one. On these pages, a hairline border, square corners, no shadow. | Medium | [prg.sh](https://prg.sh/ramblings/Why-Your-AI-Keeps-Building-the-Same-Purple-Gradient-Website), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Side-stripe accent | A thick coloured `border-left` on a card or callout, often a different colour per card. | Remove unless it marks a real state such as a warning. | Medium | [Fountain Institute](https://pages.thefountaininstitute.com/posts/7-tells-that-a-ui-is-ai-generated), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Glow, glass and neon | Radial halo behind content, `backdrop-filter: blur` panels, glowing borders, neon accents on dark backgrounds. | Remove. Use spacing and contrast for focus. | Medium | [Fountain Institute](https://pages.thefountaininstitute.com/posts/7-tells-that-a-ui-is-ai-generated), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Decorative status dots | Coloured or pulsing dots next to items that have no live state. | Keep dots only for real status, and add a text label. | Medium | [Fountain Institute](https://pages.thefountaininstitute.com/posts/7-tells-that-a-ui-is-ai-generated), [UX Skill dashboard wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-design-a-dashboard-with-Claude-Code) |
| Unfocused palette | Five or more saturated colours with no dominant one, or the accent used on nav, links, borders, badges and chart series at once. | One accent for one job. Neutrals for everything else. | Medium | [Fountain Institute](https://pages.thefountaininstitute.com/posts/7-tells-that-a-ui-is-ai-generated), [Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Flat type hierarchy | Headline and body differ only slightly (400 against 600 weight, 18 px against 24 px); default Tailwind size steps. | Clear jumps in size and weight between headline, body and caption. | Medium | [Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Default font by reflex | Inter, Roboto, Arial, Geist, Space Grotesk or Plus Jakarta Sans chosen with no reason. | Use the house font. | Medium | [Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills), [prg.sh](https://prg.sh/ramblings/Why-Your-AI-Keeps-Building-the-Same-Purple-Gradient-Website), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Decorative motion | Fade-in on every block, shimmer on hover, drifting blobs, bounce easing, auto-scrolling marquees, content invisible until an animation fires. | Motion only where it answers a question (state change, reveal of a result). Content visible by default. | Medium | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [prg.sh](https://prg.sh/ramblings/Why-Your-AI-Keeps-Building-the-Same-Purple-Gradient-Website), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Monotonous spacing and centring | Equal gaps between all blocks, every section the same height, every text block centred; heading closer to the block above than to its own content. | Tight within groups, loose between them. Left-align text. More space above a heading than below. | Medium | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Stock "tasteful" look applied regardless of subject | Cream background (near #F4F1EA) with italic serif display and terracotta accent; or near-black with one acid green accent; or broadsheet columns with hairlines everywhere. | Check each choice serves this content. Keep the house look but do not add newspaper flourishes for their own sake. | Medium | [DEV Community (kenimo49)](https://dev.to/kenimo49/anthropic-rewrote-frontend-design-skill-3-ai-design-cliches-named-with-hex-codes-22m4), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Happy path only | No empty, loading or error state; broken or placeholder image; console errors on load. | Design each state. Fix errors before reviewing looks. | Medium | [UX Skill dashboard wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-design-a-dashboard-with-Claude-Code), [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |
| Line length and ragged headings | Body lines over 75 characters; headlines over about 45 characters per line; a single orphan word on a last line. | Cap body at 50 to 75 characters; `text-wrap: balance` on headlines, `pretty` on body. | Low | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop), [UX Skill wiki](https://github.com/Laith0003/ux-skill/wiki/How-to-detect-AI-slop-in-your-design) |
| Filler backgrounds and illustrations | Decorative grid lines, stripes, blobs or rough SVG mascots that carry no data. | Remove. Plain white ground. | Low | [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/slop) |

## Conflicts with the house rules

The house rules are the Writing style and Design sections of `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`. Where an item below overlaps with them, the house rule wins.

| Checklist item | Relation to the house rule | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Em dash habit | Duplicates the rule "never use em-dashes". The house rule is stricter (zero, not "fewer"). | House rule wins. Any em dash fails. The project lexicon also bans en dashes in visible strings. |
| Bold label lists ("**Label**: text") | Duplicates the no-colons-in-prose rule. | House rule wins. Colons in prose fail even without bold. |
| Signposting, "The result?" and reveal phrases | Overlap with the colon rule, which also bans "Here's the key insight: ..." style reveals. | House rule wins. |
| Kicker, badge or number above a title | Duplicates the Design rule (no kicker or label above a title, no status tags or numbering). | House rule wins. |
| Caption that restates the headline | Duplicates the Design rule (no note under a label unless the reader needs it, no comments that state the obvious). | House rule wins. |
| Uniform staccato rhythm | Partly in tension. Wikipedia reports newer models favour short sentences and commas over dashes, and the house rules push toward separate sentences. | House rule wins. Splitting sentences is correct; the critic should only flag runs of same-length fragments, never a split made to avoid a colon or dash. |
| AI vocabulary and "-ing" tails (US spellings in sources) | Sources list "emphasizing", "optimize" and so on. | British spelling applies to every word that survives; flag both spellings. |
| Fixes in the Anthropic and prg.sh sources (distinctive display font, layered gradient backgrounds, orchestrated page-load animation) | Contradict Kilian's chosen editorial look for the model pages (white ground, SF Pro only, one indigo accent, square corners, hairlines, no shadows). | Kilian's choice wins. Do not suggest new fonts, textures or entrance animations. |
| Purple gradient and "default font" items | The house accent is indigo and the house font is a system font, both flagged by the sources as AI defaults when used by reflex. | Kilian's choice wins. Flag only gradients, indigo used as decoration beyond its one job, or any font other than SF Pro. |
| Stock "tasteful" look (broadsheet hairlines, zero radius) | Describes part of the house look. | Kilian's choice wins. Flag only added newspaper flourishes (drop caps, dense multi-column text, decorative rules). |
| Equal-weight KPI row | Kilian asked for the opening boxes back and kept the Results boxes (clustering page review, 2026-10-31). | Kilian's most recent choice wins. Rows of equal number boxes are allowed. |
| Hero metric with no meaning | Kilian removed the comparison notes under the number boxes and wants bare subject-noun labels. | Kilian's most recent choice wins. A box is a number and a label; the comparison lives in the chart or drawer. |
| Accessibility (body text under 16 px) | Captions, labels and table text on the model pages are 12 to 14 px, as approved. | Kilian's most recent choice wins on size. Contrast, focus and colour-only encoding still apply. |
| Decorative status dots | Kilian kept the type-colour dots in the feature table headers. | Kilian's most recent choice wins. Colour dots that identify a series or group are allowed. |

Decided by Kilian at Gate 0 (2026-11-01). Where a checklist sign clashes with a choice he made, his most recent choice wins.
