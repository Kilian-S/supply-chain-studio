## The storyline as a whole

The arc (context, split, methods, validation, forecast, error by year, uncertainty, decision) fits the opening question and is honest in intent. It keeps validation before the test, keeps the method fixed after the test and keeps model quality apart from business value. As written, it does not yet give a clear, honest answer. The horizon it states does not match the default view (1). The uncertainty screen measures against a ground truth that leaves out launches and growth-end ages, and reads 100% as success (3). The business result picks the one simulation in which our plan loses (4). The test-informed changes are under-disclosed and come too late (5). For an operations research audience, two things are missing. One is the life-cycle picture that sets up the reversal (17). The other is a plain statement of what the setting flatters (15). Superfluous are the bias table on screen 5 (19) and, unless Kilian wants them, the 24 fan states on screen 3 (16, 17).

Verdict. FAIL, 5 high, 15 medium, 9 low.
