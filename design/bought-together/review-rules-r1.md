## Open decisions

1. **Vocabulary.** Approve "community". It is the standard name for what Louvain returns and keeps "cluster" for the clustering page, while "group" is vaguer and collides with the feature groups there. Promote "lift" too, as the standard market-basket term and shorter than any paraphrase. That is the move Kilian made with "feature space". The fallback is "Times chance" on every surface. Add "accepted pair" to the same question. It is honest and has no plain fallback that keeps its meaning. Keep "hidden bundles" on the surface and say once in the screen 5 drawer that they are the ground truth, so both pages share one term underneath.
2. **Baseline for the result boxes.** Keep Category. It is the strongest plain rule and what a shop would do without the model, which matches the clustering page comparing with established practice (ABC/XYZ). Random stays visible in the bars and the drawer table. Quoting the larger figure against Random would mean picking the baseline that flatters, which rule 6 warns against.
3. **Screen 2 and PairMatrix.** Keep it. Counting pairs is the obvious first method and showing why it fails is its own storyline step (rule 5). The matrix is the dense, direct encoding of real values Kilian chose on the clustering feature table (rule 7). The component also pays for itself twice, because the screen 4 drawer needs it for the community blocks. Dropping it would make screen 3 introduce pairs, counts and chance at once, two ideas on one screen. This holds only with the fixes in rows 6 to 9.
