Job Description
The Senior Operations Research function identifies and structures complex operational problems, collects and analyzes relevant data, and applies appropriate analytical methods to develop practical, scalable solutions. It works with stakeholders to understand processes, constraints, and objectives, while using AI and advanced analytics to improve decision-making and automation. The function also communicates insights, recommendations, and best practices clearly to managers and executives.
Your responsibilities 

Support teams and departments in building data‑driven decision support tools, models, and algorithms 

Provide advanced analytical insights to the Assortment Excellence team, with focus on assortment quality, connectivity, segmentation, and performance 

Support the Listing team in assortment allocation by improving algorithms, increasing automation, and enhancing scalability 

Collaborate with Procurement, Replenishment, and Inventory teams to design, improve, and maintain algorithms (e.g. safety stock allocation, sourcing‑channel decisions, inventory positioning) 

Act as a subject‑matter expert for analytical methods, optimization, simulation, and AI‑based approaches in operations

Qualifications

University degree with a strong quantitative focus, such as Engineering, Mathematics, Physics, Economics, Business Administration, Operations Research, Data Science, or a comparable field 

Proven experience in mathematical modeling and structured problem solving, ideally including hands‑on work with optimization solvers (e.g. Gurobi or comparable solver engines) 

Strong programming skills in Python for analytical modeling, automation, and prototyping (experience with AI‑assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot is a plus) 

Advanced skills in SQL and Excel for fast, ad‑hoc analyses and exploratory data work 

Demonstrated ability to process, analyze, and interpret large and complex datasets, and to derive actionable business insights 

Strong organizational skills with the ability to work independently, prioritize effectively, and drive topics proactively 

Assertive personality with a structured, goal‑oriented, and solution‑focused mindset 

Excellent analytical and communication skills, with high attention to detail and a strong commitment to quality and excellence 

Fluent in English; German language skills are an advantage, but not mandatory
